01FEATURES

Your words, typeset.

Instead of streaming a raw transcript, KANPE picks out the keywords and sets them on screen with real typographic care. Designed around Japanese script — and it works in English, too.

01

Kanji stand tall, kana step back

Kanji are set bold and large; kana endings shrink to about 80% and get lighter. Katakana go a touch larger because they read small. Every script gets its own treatment.

02
ideaideaidea

Say it again, and it grows

Each time you repeat a word it gets bigger and bolder, so the topic of the moment is obvious at a glance.

03
New idea

A small reward for new words

The first time a standout word comes up in a stream, it arrives in the accent color with an underline — at most one every five seconds, so it stays special.

04

No “um”s on screen

Fillers like “um”, “uh” and “you know” are kept off the screen and out of the transcript. Stumble all you like — the screen stays clean.

Talk a lot and the screen gets busy; go quiet and words linger, slowly fading. The screen breathes with the rhythm of your talk.

06PRIVACY

What you say stays on your device.

KANPE has no server that receives your transcripts or settings. Data moves as little as possible, so you can stream with peace of mind.

  • LOCALNothing kept on a server

    Transcripts, settings and background images are stored only in your own browser. KANPE never receives or stores them.

  • BROWSERRecognition by your browser

    On Free and Plus, speech recognition is built into your browser. Your browser’s vendor turns audio into text (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, Apple in Safari) — it never reaches KANPE.

  • DIRECTScreens talk directly

    The console and display views talk to each other inside the same browser, and OBS connects straight to your own OBS. Your OBS password is never sent to a server.

  • ERASEDelete any time

    Clear the transcript with the Clear button; clear your browser’s site data to remove everything else. On a shared computer, clear it when you’re done.

All traffic is encrypted (https). The app itself (console and display view) carries no ads, analytics tags or third-party scripts; only this introduction page and the legal pages use Google Analytics to see how they are found and read (nothing you say is ever sent). Pro’s cloud recognition (coming soon) will send audio only to be transcribed and is designed so nothing is stored on KANPE’s servers.

07FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free version runs entirely on your browser’s built-in speech recognition and keyword extraction in the page, so there’s nothing to pay and no time limit.

Which browsers work? Does it work on my phone?

Voice input works in Chrome and Edge on desktop, Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. Allow microphone access when asked. On iPhone, Siri (or Dictation) needs to be turned on in Settings. In-app browsers such as those in LINE or X aren’t supported — reopen the page in Safari or Chrome. The display views alone work in almost any browser, including OBS Browser Sources.

How do I use it with OBS?

Recognition runs in the console in Chrome and the results are sent to OBS. With OBS’s built-in WebSocket server (OBS 28 and later) you can overlay it with a transparent background. For a quick start, you can also capture the display window with OBS’s Window Capture.

Is what I say stored anywhere?

Nothing is stored on KANPE’s servers. Transcripts, settings and background images live only in your own browser on this device, and you can delete them with the Clear button or by clearing site data. Only the step that turns audio into text is handled by your browser vendor’s service (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, Apple in Safari). For confidential topics, blocked words keep chosen terms off the screen and out of captions.

Can I use it in meetings or talks?

Yes. Show a display view on the meeting-room screen or a lectern monitor. It picks up several voices but doesn’t tell them apart automatically (with Plus you can color-code speakers using the buttons or keys). Voices far from the mic are harder to pick up, so keep the mic near whoever is talking. For confidential meetings, check your organization’s rules first.

Where can I find detailed instructions?

Press Help at the top right of the console for getting started, a tour of the screen, recommended settings by scene, OBS setup and troubleshooting. Open the guide

How do payment and cancellation work?

You pay on Stripe’s page by card or other methods — card details never pass through KANPE. Choose monthly or yearly; plans renew automatically. Cancel any time from “Your plan” → “Billing, cancel, receipts” in the console, and keep access until the end of the period (no prorated refunds). To use your plan on another device, or after clearing your browser, restore it with your license code. The code is shown right after you subscribe and also emailed to the address you subscribed with. Lost it? Have it emailed again from “Your plan” → “Restore with a license code” in the console.

What’s the difference between Plus and Pro?

Plus (¥480/mo) bundles the looks and effects — VJ mode, speaker colors, premium typefaces, background images, a custom logo — and keeps unlimited browser speech recognition. Pro (¥2,980/mo) adds 20 hours a month of high-accuracy cloud recognition, enough for three 2-hour streams a week, plus automatic speaker separation, AI key points and post-stream summaries.

Can it recognize English speech?

Yes. Choose English as the speech language in the console (it’s the default when you open the English version). Keyword extraction and filler removal work in English as well as Japanese.
