Privacy policy

What information KANPE handles, and how.

Our approach

KANPE is designed to collect as little as possible so you can use it with confidence. Transcripts, settings, background images and OBS connection details are stored only in your browser and are never sent to or stored on our servers. There are no accounts.

Speech recognition

On the Free and Plus plans, speech recognition uses your browser’s built-in feature. Audio is converted to text by your browser’s provider (Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, Apple for Safari) under their privacy policies. Audio never reaches us.

Information for paid plans

Payments are handled on the checkout page of Stripe, Inc. Stripe receives and manages your email address, name and card details; we never receive your card details. To verify your subscription, we look up your Stripe customer ID, plan, subscription period and sign-up email from Stripe when needed. We do not keep these in a database of our own; the result is stored in your browser as a signed license code. To deliver your license code, we email it to your sign-up address through Resend, Inc., an email delivery service. If you ask us to resend the code, the address you enter is used only to look up a matching subscription, and the email is sent only to the address registered with Stripe.

Access logs

The Service is delivered via Cloudflare, Inc. For security and reliability, Cloudflare may temporarily process technical information such as IP addresses and browser type. We use no advertising tags; the only third-party script is Google Analytics, described under “Analytics” below.

Analytics

Our introduction pages (the home page, the legal pages and the page-not-found notice) use Google Analytics 4 from Google LLC to understand how they are found and read. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to send Google information such as the pages viewed, the referring site, approximate location, device and browser type, and button clicks. We send nothing that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address, and we do not use advertising features (Google signals or ad personalization).
Google Analytics is not used in the app itself (the console and display view), so what you say, your transcripts and your settings are never sent. It is not loaded if your browser asks not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track), and no cookies are used for visits from the EU, the UK or Switzerland.
To opt out, use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For how Google handles this information, see Google’s privacy policy.

Purpose of use

We use the information we access only to provide paid plans and verify subscriptions, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse, and improve the Service through analytics.

Sharing with third parties

Except as required by law, we do not share personal information with third parties without your consent. Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (delivery), Resend (email) and Google (analytics) handle information only as needed for their services; all include providers outside Japan.

Data stored in your browser

Your browser stores settings, transcripts and your license code. You can delete them at any time with the console’s Clear button or by clearing site data in your browser. If you delete your license code, you can restore it with “Restore with a license code”.

Your rights

We respond to requests for disclosure, correction, suspension or deletion in accordance with the law. You can also view and update the information held by Stripe from the billing page.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post changes on this page.

For enquiries, please use the contact listed in the Legal notice.
